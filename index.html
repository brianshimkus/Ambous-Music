About

Where It Started

Ambous Music started with a simple idea: play guitar, chase the sound in your head, and never let fear get in the way of finishing the song. What began as late nights with a beat-up amp has grown into a project built on ambition, curiosity, and a refusal to sound like everyone else.

Every riff, take, and rough demo shared here comes from the same place — a love of the instrument and the belief that the best music happens when you stop overthinking and just play.
